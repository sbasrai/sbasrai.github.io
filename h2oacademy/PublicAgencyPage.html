On‑Site Water Operator Training

Sessions can be customized in both duration and topics, incorporating your agency’s input and site‑specific information.

3‑Hour: Water Math Essentials

  • Audience: T1/D1–T2/D2; CEU‑qualifying refresher
  • Unit conversions
  • Flow & volume; detention time
  • Dosage & feed rate (mg/L, lbs/day)
  • Practice: 10–15 problems

6‑Hour: Applied Water Math

  • Audience: T2–T3 / D2–D3
  • Includes 3‑hour topics
  • Pressure & head; hydraulics
  • Pumping & horsepower; energy cost
  • Efficiency & percent calculations
  • Practice: 20–25 problems

9‑Hour: Certification Prep Intensive

  • Audience: T3–T5 / D3–D5
  • Advanced feed rate & CT
  • Loading & filtration rates
  • BOD & organic load
  • Timed exam‑style sets (30+ problems)
