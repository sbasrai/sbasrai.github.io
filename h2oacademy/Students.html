Launch or Advance Your Career in Water

At Bassett Adult School, I teach a 16‑session, 3.5‑hour per‑class Water Technology course designed to prepare you for the State Water Resources Control Board (SWRCB) certification.

What You’ll Gain

  • 36‑specified Training Certificate (35 hours) in Treatment or Distribution—meeting higher‑level certification
  • CEU Credits for certification renewal
  • Comprehensive Curriculum covering fundamentals and advanced topics
  • Professional Textbook for exam prep and growth opportunities

Whether you’re starting fresh or moving up the ranks, this program equips you with the knowledge, credentials, and confidence to succeed.
